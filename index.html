09 · FAQ

Questions engineers ask first

Can I use MoldAid today?

MoldAid is in private beta. Public release is January 2027. Join the waitlist for early access.

Do I need my own printer and molding machine?

MoldAid is built for teams that print and mold in-house. If you don't have the equipment, tell us when you sign up and we'll help.

Which file formats do you accept?

You upload a STEP file and download the mold as STEP and STL. Part size is limited only by your printer.

Can I use my own resin?

Yes. Add your resin's data and MoldAid uses it for the flow check and cost estimate. Two resins are built in if you don't have your own.

What does it cost?

You pay per mold you download. The early-access price is from €99 per download.

What happens to my CAD files?

They stay yours. We only access them, or use them for training or statistics, with your explicit permission. See our privacy policy.
